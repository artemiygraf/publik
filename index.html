About Publik

A marketing agency producing short-form video and smart NFC engagement tools, built to order.

We produce short promotional videos for clients by combining our own filming with AI video tools, and design custom NFC cards that let customers tap to leave a review, follow a business's social media, or reach its website. Every project is scoped and delivered per order.
